# Aurelia Smart City Website

A responsive, zero-dependency Smart City web portal showcasing real-time urban infrastructure, citizen services, and civic telemetry.

## Included Modules
1. **City Statistics & Live Telemetry**: Real-time AQI, grid load, traffic flow, population, renewable energy share, and 2030 Vision toggle.
2. **Transportation & Live Transit**: Filterable Metro corridors, Electric Bus routes, EV superchargers, and Smart Bike docks.
3. **Hospitals & Healthcare**: Live general/ICU bed counts, 24/7 trauma centers, and specialty filters.
4. **Education & Digital Libraries**: Universities, smart K–12 schools, and public digital makerspaces.
5. **Public Services (E-Governance)**: Digital utility/certificate portals plus an interactive Citizen Service Ticket submission form.
6. **Emergency Information & SOS**: Top hotline bar (`112`, `100`, `108`, `101`, `1077`), emergency response directory, and an interactive SOS Dispatch modal.
7. **Important Locations & Interactive Sector Map**: Clickable city map pins with live landmark details and category filters.
